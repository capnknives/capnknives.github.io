Mortals and Monsters

About

Ordinary American towns. Hunters work cases. Older things wear other people's faces. Type to play. The world does not pause when you log off.

What this is

A persistent Supernatural-flavored text MUD. You read rooms and type commands. Your body stays as an Echo when you leave. Nobody loots you for going to dinner.

Who it is for

18+. Horror and violence are part of the world. Roleplay MUD, not hard RPI: level, trade, explore, hang out. Free. No pay-to-play and no pay-for-perks.

How you get in

Play in your browser. Same login as telnet. Discord is the OOC hall.
