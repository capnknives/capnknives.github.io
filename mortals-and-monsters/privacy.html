Mortals and Monsters

Privacy

What the game stores. Not legal advice.

What we keep

  • Character name and a salted password hash — not the password itself.
  • Game state: your character, inventory, location, prefs, and Echo while you play.
  • Bug and suggestion text you file in-game, plus recent commands used to reproduce issues.
  • Server logs that may include connection timing and errors. Staff do not routinely read command history except for abuse or a bug you reported.

What we do not do

We do not sell player data. We do not run third-party ad trackers in the telnet or browser client.

Your choices

Use a unique password. Ask staff to delete or anonymize a report that contains personal information.
